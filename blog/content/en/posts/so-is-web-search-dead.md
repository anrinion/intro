---

Google has just [announced](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/19/google-search-as-you-know-it-is-over/) their shift to AI-based results. This was expected. They discontinued their Search API for new users in 2024, [recommending](https://developers.google.com/custom-search/v1/overview) Vertex AI instead. [Same thing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/announcements/bing-search-api-retirement) with Bing Search API, moving to Azure AI Agents. 

That's bad news for end users. People are looking for alternatives, but most of them simply repackage Google's (StartPage) and Bing's (DuckDuckGo, Ecosia) results.

What's left:
- **Brave** remains independent (for now?) and looks like the most promising alternative for API users. 
- **Kagi** uses results from Google and Bing but maintains their own index as well.
- New competitors, e.g. the [partnership between Ecosia and Qwant](https://blog.ecosia.org/launching-our-european-search-index/) announced in 2024 and showing some early results.

There is also Yandex, though their current state is unclear. 

It will be interesting to see what LLM providers do. Their models need search APIs too. 

Interesting times, God damn it.
