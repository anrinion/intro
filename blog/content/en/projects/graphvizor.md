A lightweight browser-based Graphviz DOT editor. Unlike many others, works reasonably on mobile. Useful, when you want to create a diagram for a heated online discussion without leaving the couch.

Hosted here: [graphvizor.ansid.de](https://graphvizor.ansid.de).
