**Baseline** is a minimal, gentle self-care app companion designed for people who struggle to maintain basic level of functioning (hence the name). It aims to help with these foundations, without pressure, motivation attempts or guilt promotion.

Install via [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.anrinion.baseline).
